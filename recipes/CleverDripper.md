# Clever Dripper

![Clever Dripper](https://cdn.rawgit.com/andmos/Coffee/master/img/CleverDripper.svg)

## Single cup - Immersion & Drip

### Intro

The Clever Dripper is a hybrid brewer that combines immersion brewing with a pour-over filter. It's great for beginners and produces a clean, full-bodied cup. Always use clean water. Remember to moisten the filter.

* Ca. 250 gr water
* 18 gr freshly ground coffee, medium filter grind

### Instructions

* Moisten the paper filter and place it in the Clever Dripper. Set it on a cup or server.
* Add the coffee grounds.
* Start a timer and pour 150 grams of water over the grounds, ensuring all coffee is saturated.
* Let the coffee bloom for 45 seconds.
* Pour the remaining 100 grams of water.
* Let the coffee steep for a total of 3 minutes (or 2:15 after adding the rest of the water).
* Place the Clever Dripper on your cup/server and open the bottom valve to let the coffee drip through the filter.
* Remove the dripper once dripping is complete (around 4 minutes total).
